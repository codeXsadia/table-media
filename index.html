What is Tea?

Tea is an aromatic beverage prepared by steeping the processed or fresh leaves of the Camellia sinensis

How many kinds of tea are there? 🍵

There are 5 main types of true tea, all made from the Camellia sinensis plant:
1.🖤 Black tea
2.💚 Green tea
3.🤍 White tea
4.🟤 Oolong tea
5.🟡 Pu-erh tea

How many people in Bangladesh drink tea, according to surveys?

A 2025 nationwide field/online study estimated that roughly 55.5% of Bangladesh's population in the 16–65 age range are active tea drinkers, with around 142.8 million cups consumed every day.

Which four Asian countries have the highest tea consumption per capita?

The four Asian countries with the largest consumption are China, India, Turkey, and Vietnam in the available 2022 data:

Country Region Annual consumption (2022) Per capita
China Asia 14.5 million tonnes 10.19 kg
India Asia 6.0 million tonnes 4.21 kg
